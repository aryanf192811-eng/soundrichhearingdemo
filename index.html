Clinical Services

Comprehensive Audiology Solutions

From detailed assessments to advanced rehabilitation, our certified audiologists provide complete end-to-end care.

Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)

Diagnostic Evaluation
Complete audiological evaluation to determine the exact degree and type of hearing loss.

Tympanometry

Middle Ear Analysis
Objective middle ear examination to diagnose issues like fluid or eardrum perforations.

Oto Acoustic Emissions (OAE)

Cochlear Function Test
A quick, non-invasive test used to determine inner ear (cochlear) health and function.

BERA / ABR Test

Neurological Test
Objective neuro-physiological evaluation of the hearing pathway from the ear to the brain.

Hearing Aid Trials

Rehabilitation
Unbiased, multi-brand hearing aid trials and precise fittings for optimal comfort.
